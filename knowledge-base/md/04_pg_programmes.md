## General PG Eligibility

- Most PG programs: Bachelor's in relevant field with **minimum 50%**.
- Ph.D. programs: PG with **minimum 55–60%** depending on school.
- Entrance: ITM NEST, GATE, CAT, CMAT, CUET PG, or merit as applicable.
