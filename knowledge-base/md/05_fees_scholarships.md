## Application Fee

- Application fee (2026 indicative): **₹1,100** for all categories.
- Apply online: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/admission/onlineapply

## General Fee Policies

- **Caution money:** ₹5,000 (refundable after course completion).
- **No capitation fee:** ITM states it does not charge capitation beyond declared fee structure; always collect official receipt.
- Fees shown for Indian students; subject to regulatory approval.
- Indicative ranges below — confirm exact branch/year on official PDF.
