## Scholarships 2026 — Full Details

Source: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/scholarship/scholarship_2026  
Enquiries: **7773005063** | admission@itmuniversity.ac.in

### 1. Entry-Level Scholarships

| Scholarship | Benefit | Eligibility |
|-------------|---------|-------------|
| **Early Bird** | Up to **25%** fee concession in 1st year | Admission confirmed before cut-off date; admission year only |
| **CUET** | **10%** course fee concession | Valid CUET scorecard; UG & PG; submit at admission |
| **ITMU NEST** | **10%** fee concession | Qualify ITMU NEST; top performers; not combinable with other scholarships |

### 2. Merit-Based Scholarships

| Benefit | Eligibility |
|---------|-------------|
| Fee concessions up to **60%** | Class 12 marks (UG) / Graduation marks (PG); JEE/national/state entrance ranks; top merit list |

**Continuation requirements:** Maintain performance band, no backlogs, may shift to lower category if performance declines.

### 3. Need-Based (Humble) Scholarship

| Benefit | Eligibility |
|---------|-------------|
| **15%** fee concession | Valid Income Certificate; ITR/Bank Statement; financial need verification; limited seats |

### 4. Special Category Scholarships

| Category | Benefit |
|----------|---------|
| Military Ward | 10% fee concession |
| Alumni | ₹10,000 fee relaxation per year |
| Sibling | 10% fee concession |
| Staff Ward | As per HR policy |

### 5. Performance-Based Scholarships

| Award | Amount |
|-------|--------|
| **Chancellor Scholarship — 1st Rank** | ₹20,000 |
| **Chancellor Scholarship — 2nd Rank** | ₹15,000 |
| **Chancellor Scholarship — 3rd Rank** | ₹10,000 |
| **Sports Scholarship** | Up to **100%** fee waiver (exceptional); hostel & financial incentives for National/International participation |

### Scholarship Conditions (All Types)

- Minimum **75% attendance**
- No academic backlogs
- Satisfactory academic performance
- Good discipline and conduct

### SWAYAM/NPTEL Fee Reimbursement

- Examination fees reimbursed as scholarship after successful NPTEL/SWAYAM certification (per university policy)
- Details: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/guidelines-provision/moocs-and-nptel-courses
