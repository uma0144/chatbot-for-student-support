## NPTEL & SWAYAM (MOOCs)

**Page:** https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/guidelines-provision/moocs-and-nptel-courses

### What are NPTEL & SWAYAM?

- **NPTEL** — National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning (IITs & IISc); free technical courses online
- **SWAYAM** — Ministry of Education initiative; free courses from school to PG level
- **1000+ courses** from IITs, IISc, and top institutes

### Benefits for ITM Students

- Skill enhancement beyond regular syllabus
- Industry-relevant certifications
- **Credit transfer:** up to **40%** of degree credits via UGC-approved SWAYAM/NPTEL courses
- **Exam fee reimbursement** as scholarship after successful certification (per university policy)
- Transportation to exam centres within Gwalior arranged by university
- Dedicated **SWAYAM/NPTEL Coordinator (SPOC)** and mentor guidance

### How to Enroll

1. Visit https://swayam.gov.in or https://nptel.ac.in
2. Choose course aligned with your programme
3. Complete enrollment and attend lectures
4. Submit 75%+ assignments on SWAYAM portal
5. Appear for proctored end-term exam at ITM (university as exam centre)
6. Apply for credit transfer with certificate and marks

### Evaluation (SWAYAM)

- **30%** — assignments/quizzes on SWAYAM portal
- **70%** — end-term exam at university
- Minimum pass: **40%** (12/30 + 28/70)
- Must complete **75%** of SWAYAM assignments to sit end-term exam

## Value Added Courses (VAC)

**Policy PDF:** https://resources.itmuniversity.ac.in/newweb/pdf/vac/PagesfromTeachingandLearningPolicy.pdf

### What is VAC?

- **Non-credit** courses beyond the regular curriculum
- Focus: technical skills, aptitude, life skills, soft skills, online platform courses
- **Not counted in SGPA/CGPA** — certificate only
- Typically **30 hours** duration with internal assessment
- Course code format: `XXXXXVACXX` (e.g. SOET VAC courses)

### VAC Guidelines

- Every department offers at least one value-added course per semester
- Course list displayed on university website with syllabus
- Registration via Head of Department (registration form)
- Students with **CGPA > 7.5** may pursue additional SWAYAM/MOOC/NPTEL courses through self-directed learning

### VAC vs NPTEL

| | VAC | NPTEL/SWAYAM |
|---|-----|--------------|
| Credit | Non-credit | Up to 40% credit transfer |
| Run by | ITM departments | IITs/IISc/MoE |
| Assessment | Internal | SWAYAM + university end-term |
| Certificate | ITM | NPTEL/SWAYAM |

## Anti-Ragging Policy

**Page:** https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/guidelines-provision/anti-ragging-committee  
**Email:** antiragging@itmuniversity.ac.in  
**Chairman:** Dr. Mukesh Pandey, Dean SOET — Dean.soet@itmuniversity.ac.in

### Key Points

- Per **UGC Regulations 2009** and Supreme Court directives
- **Zero tolerance** for ragging in any form
- Anti-Ragging Committee + Anti-Ragging Squad constituted
- **Complaint:** Written or email to antiragging@itmuniversity.ac.in within 1 month of incident
- **Online undertaking mandatory** at www.antiragging.in after admission
- **CCTV** coverage across campus and hostels
- Orientation for students and parents on anti-ragging laws

### Punishments (if found guilty)

- Suspension, fine up to ₹25,000, hostel expulsion, rustication (1–4 semesters)
- Cancellation of admission
- Imprisonment up to 2 years (legal provisions)
- Collective punishment if individuals not identified
