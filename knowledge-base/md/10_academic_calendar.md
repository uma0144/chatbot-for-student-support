## Typical Academic Year Structure

- **Semester system** — two semesters per academic year (odd/even).
- **Internal assessments** during semester (Class Participation 20%, Mid-Term 20%).
- **End-semester examinations** at end of each semester (60% weightage).
- **Registration** at start of each semester via MIS (Prabandh).
- **Attendance requirement:** minimum **75%** per course for exam eligibility.

## Examination Periods (Recent Examples)

End-semester exams are typically held in:
- **June** (even semesters — II, IV, VI, VIII, X)
- **December / March** (odd semesters and yearly courses)

Recent exam cycles (2025-2026):
- B.Tech, BCA, MCA, BBA, B.Com, MBA, B.Pharm, B.Sc., Law, Nursing, Agriculture — June 2026
- Yearly courses (B.Ed, BPT, etc.) — March 2026
- Special/passout batches — July 2026
