ITM University uses **continuous evaluation** throughout the semester plus an **end-semester examination**.

## SGPA & CGPA

- **SGPA** = (Σ Ci × Pi) / Σ Ci — semester grade point average
- **CGPA** — cumulative across all semesters from first to present

## Attendance & Detainment

- **Minimum 75% attendance** required per course for end-semester exam eligibility.
- Students below 75% may be **detained** and not allowed to sit for end-semester exams.

## Revaluation & Grievances

- **Revaluation / re-totalling:** Apply within **one week** of result declaration.
- **Grade card errors:** Report to Controller of Examinations within **15 days**.

## SWAYAM / NPTEL Exams

- End-term SWAYAM exams conducted **at university** as designated centre.
- **70%** end-term exam + **30%** SWAYAM assignments/quizzes.
- Minimum pass: **40%** overall (12/30 assessments + 28/70 end-term).
- Must complete **75%** of SWAYAM assignments to sit end-term exam.
- Details: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/guidelines-provision/moocs-and-nptel-courses
