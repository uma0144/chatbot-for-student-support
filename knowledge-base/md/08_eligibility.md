# Eligibility Criteria — All Courses | ITM University Gwalior

Source: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/admission/eligibility  
**Always verify on the official eligibility page before applying.**

## General Rules

- Eligibility is based on **10+2** marks for UG and **graduation** marks for PG.
- **Reserved categories** often get 5% relaxation (e.g. B.Tech 50% → 45%).
- **Honours** programmes typically require **55%+** in qualifying exam.
- Selection via **ITM NEST**, **JEE Main**, **NEET**, **GATE**, **CAT**, **CMAT**, **CUET**, **NATA**, or **merit** depending on programme.
