# Campus & Infrastructure — ITM University Gwalior

Source: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/academics/infrastructure

## Campus Overview

- **Location:** Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh — lush green campus on NH-44
- **Motto:** Celebrating Dreams
- **Features:** World-class sculptures, smart classrooms, research labs, hospital, hostels, sports complex, auditoriums

**Infrastructure page:** https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/academics/infrastructure

## Laboratories (by School)

### School of Engineering & Technology (SOET)

**Electronics/Communication:** Control Systems, Digital Electronics, Microprocessor & Microcontroller, Semiconductor Devices, Sensors & IoT, Antenna & Microwave, Simulation

**Computer Science:** Programming Labs, Web Programming, OOP, Cyber Security, Data Science, AI, Database Management

**Mechanical:** Fluid Machinery, CNC, Automobile, Fluid Mechanics, Strength of Materials

**Civil:** Highway & Traffic Engineering, Concrete Technology

**Electrical:** Electrical Measurements, Renewable Energy, Electrical Machines, Basic Electrical Engineering
