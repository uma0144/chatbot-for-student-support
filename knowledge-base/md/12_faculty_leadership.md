## Teaching Approach

- Project-Based Learning (PBL), Problem-Based Learning, flipped classrooms
- Industry immersion programmes with MoU partners
- Mandatory **AI** and **IKS (Indian Knowledge Systems)** courses for all students
- ABCA continuous assessment; NPTEL/SWAYAM integration
- Research labs in nanotechnology, biotechnology, forensic science, agriculture, and engineering
