# Hostel Facilities — ITM University Gwalior

Source: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/admission/faq and official fee structure.

## Overview

ITM University Gwalior provides **on-campus hostel facilities** for boys and girls with options for **AC** and **non-AC** rooms, mess, and 24×7 security.

## Hostel Types

| Type | Features |
|------|----------|
| **Boys Hostel** | On-campus residential blocks with study areas |
| **Girls Hostel** | Separate secure accommodation for female students |
| **AC Hostel** | Air-conditioned rooms (higher fee tier) |
| **Non-AC Hostel** | Standard ventilated rooms (lower fee tier) |

## Facilities

- **Mess / dining** — hostel mess for daily meals
- **Security** — campus and hostel security personnel
- **CCTV vigilance** — campus-wide CCTV including vulnerable areas
- **Wi-Fi / smart infrastructure** — as per campus norms
- **Proximity to academic blocks** — walking distance to classrooms and labs
- **Student Bazar** — on-campus convenience store
- **Guest House** — for visitors (separate from student hostels)

## Fee (Indicative)

- **Hostel total fee:** approximately **₹85,000 – ₹3,08,000** depending on AC/non-AC and duration.
- Exact figures are in the official Fee Structure PDF: https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/admission/fee-structure
- **Caution money:** ₹5,000 (refundable after course completion — separate from hostel fee).

## Rules & Policies

- Students must follow **hostel rules** issued at admission (discipline, timings, visitors).
- **Anti-ragging undertaking** mandatory at admission; online form at www.antiragging.in
- Anti-ragging squad conducts **surprise raids** on hostels (per UGC regulations).
- **75% attendance** required in academics to remain in good standing (linked to exam eligibility).
- Grievances: Students' Grievance Redressal Committee — https://www.itmuniversity.ac.in/guidelines-provision/students-grievance-redressal-committee
